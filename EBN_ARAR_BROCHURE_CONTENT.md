# About Us

EBN ARAR TRADING. Cont. & Transporting Co W.L.L. is a leading company dedicated to trading services, transporting and contracting timely projects to a multitude of clientele in the state of Qatar.

We pride ourselves in the ability to provide quality and efficiency to the services we offer with realistic costs and reliable resources, both human and material.

Since its inception in 2003, EBN ARAR Group has expanded into several divisions, each with its own specialty in order to meet our customers' increasing demands. These divisions are fully operational and work independently and with utmost autonomy. We have broken them into three divisions which are:

1. Trading & Services
2. Engineering & Contracting
3. Heavy Equipment & Transportation

These expansion plans represent our company's vision and mission to grow into a world leader of supplies. We are looking at further expansions both externally in the markets we serve and internally in the divisions we establish.

EBN ARAR group is willing to go the distance to serve our clients' needs. Over the past years, we have taken serious measures to grow and expand around the globe. At EBN ARAR group, we believe in the power of our staff. We endeavor to be an excellent place to work, we treat our employees equally and we encourage teamwork, offering interesting, challenging and rewarding careers.

# Heavy Equipment & Transportation

Ebn Arar Heavy Equipment & Transportation division was created in response to the rising demand in the construction industry.

Ebn Arar Heavy Equipment & Transportation prides itself in servicing a large number of residential, commercial and industrial companies in Qatar. We display a wide range of heavy equipment and transportation relevant to construction sites and excavation works.

Our product list includes:

- **Man lifts**
- **Forklifts**
- **Excavators**
- **Cranes**
- **Bulldozers**
- **Water tankers**

Ebn Arar Heavy Equipment & Transportation operates out of a rental and sale basis. The products displayed are not operated by the division; they are rented out/sold according to the clients' needs.

Persistent maintenance is carried out to make sure all equipment is repaired in accordance to safety standards and excellent functionality.

Our maintenance team is located on sight in our warehouses to ensure our products meet safety standards, are kept clean and in good shape.

Our commitment to quality, performance and fair pricing has given us a distinct signature in the market with an envied reputation among our competitors.

Due to the increasing market demands and clients' requests, we have plans to expand our division by acquiring new equipment and adding new products to our existing range.
